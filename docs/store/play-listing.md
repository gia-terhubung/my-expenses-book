# Google Play listing: My Expenses Book

Copy this into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. EN is the default listing. ID is a translation (Play Console → Translations → Add your own translation). ES and PT-BR listings are deferred to v1.1 (plan call C3).

- **App category:** Finance
- **Tags (pick up to 5 in Console):** Expense tracking, Receipt scanner, Productivity, Travel, Tax
- **Contact email:** CONTACT_EMAIL
- **Privacy policy URL:** the URL where `site/privacy/index.html` is published (same value as `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` in `apps/mobile/src/about/links.ts`)

## English (en-US)

**App name (≤30):** My Expenses Book

**Short description (≤80):** Scan receipts into searchable expenses. Private, offline, no account.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Scan a receipt and get a structured, searchable expense record. My Expenses Book reads the merchant, date, total, tax, currency and category for you, and you only confirm.

Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. The database and images are encrypted, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.

• Scan multi-page receipts or import photos and screenshots
• On-device reading of totals, dates and currencies in many languages
• Multi-currency: foreign receipts are converted at the rate of the purchase date
• Folders for trips and projects, plus tax-year views with totals
• Fast full-text search across merchants, notes and receipt text
• Export CSV or a PDF report for expense claims and taxes
• Encrypted backup file you keep wherever you like, with restore and merge
• Works on phones, tablets and foldables

The app uses the network to download exchange-rate tables (a currency code and a date, never your data) and for the optional tip jar through Google Play. On-device text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which sends Google anonymous diagnostics (never your receipts).

## Bahasa Indonesia (id)

**Nama aplikasi:** My Expenses Book

**Deskripsi singkat:** Pindai struk jadi catatan pengeluaran. Privat, offline, tanpa akun.

**Deskripsi lengkap:**

Pindai struk dan dapatkan catatan pengeluaran yang rapi dan bisa dicari. My Expenses Book membaca nama toko, tanggal, total, pajak, mata uang, dan kategori secara otomatis, Anda tinggal mengonfirmasi.

Semuanya tetap di ponsel Anda. Tanpa akun, tanpa iklan, tanpa pelacakan. Database dan gambar dienkripsi, dan aplikasi bisa dikunci dengan sidik jari, wajah, atau PIN perangkat.

• Pindai struk beberapa halaman atau impor foto dan tangkapan layar
• Membaca total, tanggal, dan mata uang di perangkat, dalam banyak bahasa
• Multi mata uang: struk asing dikonversi dengan kurs tanggal pembelian
• Folder untuk perjalanan dan proyek, plus tampilan tahun pajak dengan total
• Pencarian cepat di nama toko, catatan, dan teks struk
• Ekspor CSV atau laporan PDF untuk klaim dan pajak
• File cadangan terenkripsi yang Anda simpan di mana saja, bisa dipulihkan atau digabung
• Berjalan di ponsel, tablet, dan perangkat lipat

Aplikasi memakai jaringan untuk mengunduh tabel kurs (kode mata uang dan tanggal, bukan data Anda) dan untuk kotak tip opsional lewat Google Play. Pengenalan teks di perangkat memakai Google ML Kit, yang mengirim data diagnostik anonim ke Google (bukan struk Anda).
