Spending stats

Stats in the Ledger slip language. The slip on Struk gains a pace line, a category strip and a way in; a new Statistik screen opens from it. Everything is bars made of plain rectangles. No budgets, forecasts or guesses.

The slip stays the loud part

Stats on the slip print in one ink, like a till receipt: white at four strengths for the category strip, pale green for supporting words. Statistik itself is quiet, on the normal background.

Up is not bad, down is not good

The pace figure is white and bold with a plain triangle. No red, no celebration green. It always says which days it compares.

Rectangles only

Every chart is a row or column of Views with a height or flex value. No SVG, no chart library. The only icons are two chevrons.

Contrast

Every text pair is 4.5:1 or more in light and dark (lowest 4.7:1). Pairs are listed in a comment at the top of this file.

Home slip

Both totals stay where they are. Below them: this month against last month at the same day, a strip of the top three categories plus Lainnya, and a foot row with the unconverted count and the way into Statistik. The whole slip is one touch target. Banner, list and floating bar are unchanged.

How it is built
  • Switch: two Pressables of 48 dp in a tonal track.
  • Month bars: six columns, each a View with a pixel height. October is two Views with a 2 dp gap.
  • Category bars: a tonal track with a green fill, width as a share of the largest category. The percentage is the share of the period total.
  • Merchants and folders reuse the feed row with its dashed tear line.
  • Folders list only those with receipts in the period, with the amount for that period.
  • Belum dikonversi uses brass, the colour for things waiting on you, and is hidden when the count is zero.

Statistik, Tahun pajak

Same screen, other segment. The chart shows all twelve months of the tax year so the year's shape is visible; December is an empty slot, not a prediction. The pace line compares with 2025 up to the same date, and the full 2025 total is printed beside it.

Differences from Bulan
  • Twelve narrow bars instead of six. Values are whole millions so they fit above each bar at 360 dp.
  • Categories, merchants and folders cover the tax year.
  • Month names follow the tax-year start set in Pengaturan.

Sparse state

First week: three receipts and no October. The pace line says so and makes no comparison. The strip shows only the categories that exist. Months without receipts are empty slots. Sections with nothing in them (folders, unconverted) are left out.
