Ledger slip

A bolder take on the polish pass. The app is a book of receipts, so the one loud element is the totals header, printed as a green till slip with a torn edge. Everything else gets quieter and more exact: tinted borders instead of grey shadows, tonal buttons, brass for things waiting on you. Element positions and wording stay as they are.

Colour

Ledger green carries actions and the slip. Brass marks drafts and warnings. Backgrounds lean green-grey, not neutral.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for totals, screen titles and month headings, bundled with the app so it works offline. Body stays the system font. All amounts use tabular figures so columns line up.

Surfaces

Three levels with meaning: the slip (loud), raised (FAB, primary button, sheet) with a green-tinted shadow, and flat rows outlined in a green hairline. No grey drop shadow on every card.

Contrast

Every text pair checked at 4.5:1 or more in light and dark (lowest 5.2:1). Touch targets 48 dp or more.

Feed

The totals become the slip, in the same place, and never break mid-number. The draft banner turns brass. Rows get a dashed tear line between them. The tab bar floats as a frosted capsule with icons, and the add button sits beside it as its own round button (the iOS 26 "duo" layout). The list scrolls under both.
